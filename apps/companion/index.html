On long runs

Past about 60 minutes, aim for roughly 30–60 g of carbs an hour — about one gel every 30–45 min, or chews/drink to match. Short easy runs don't need it.

The steady base — fat & fibre

Carbs are what we move up and down with the training — that's the bottle. Fat, protein and fibre are the steady base: they barely flex, and they're never the thing to cut. Periodised fuelling flexes the carbs, not the base.

Around a hard session

Before and during: simple carbs, low in fibre and fat so nothing sits heavy. First recovery snack stays simple too — fast carbs and protein.

After, and away from it

Bring fibre and healthy fats back from your next proper meal on — veg, wholegrains, good fats. Away from the session is where they belong.

Why fat stays in

Hormones, bone and menstrual health, and vitamins A, D, E, K all ride on dietary fat. Keep it steady; don't let it fall when carbs drop.

Why fibre stays in

Your gut needs it daily. Cutting it to feel "light" backfires — it's timing, not removal. Keep fibre in; just don't stack it against your hardest runs.
