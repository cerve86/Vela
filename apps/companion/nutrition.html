Energy & nutrition check

A quick look at the three things that quietly hold endurance back — under-fuelling, low iron, and low vitamin D. It's a pointer, not a diagnosis, and it stays between you and your coach.

When to run this: once early in a training block — iron and vitamin D take weeks to months to put right, so there's time to act — and again in your peak weeks, when the load is highest.

A pointer, not a diagnosis — and not a referral. This flags what's worth a conversation or a simple blood test, and suggests values to discuss; your GP evaluates them against your full history. Your answers stay on this device, your coach confirms anything before your plan changes, and if eating ever feels stressful or out of your control, you deserve proper support — say the word and your coach will help you find it.
